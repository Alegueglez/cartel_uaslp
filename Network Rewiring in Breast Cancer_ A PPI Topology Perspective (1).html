Two interactomes, two architectures

Breast cancer caused 2.3 M new cases and 666 k deaths in 2022, and is projected to exceed 6 M cases a year by 2050. Tumor phenotypes emerge from coordinated protein interactions, so we compare the PPI network of normal mammary epithelium with that of Luminal A tumor.

    Triangles ×4 give bypass routes around inhibited nodes. Shorter paths let EGFR reach MYC/CCND1 in fewer steps. Lower Q means PI3K/AKT and MAPK pathways entangle.

    Functional identity swap

    ◀ NormalBreast cancer ▶

    Oncogene, DNA-repair and tumor-suppressor nodes appear only in the tumor network. ECM, lactation, differentiation and UPR programs vanish, consistent with EMT-like loss of epithelial identity.

    Robust to chance, fragile at the hubs

    We removed nodes in silico and tracked the giant component S: random failures (100 Monte Carlo runs) against targeted attacks on the highest-degree nodes.

    Therapy hypotheses

    1. Multi-target hub inhibitionPI3K + AKT + mTOR inhibitors (alpelisib, capivasertib, everolimus).
    2. Hit the bottlenecksCo-target AKT1 (hub) and CTNNB1 (bridge) to fragment the core.
    3. Restore modularityWnt/β-catenin or EMT-TF (SNAI1, ZEB1) inhibition.
    4. Topological biomarkersC̄, Q, Eglob alongside ER, PR, HER2, Ki-67.
    5. Disrupt PPI interfacesAKT1/CTNNB1 surfaces: more specific than ATP-competitive drugs.

    Hypotheses from topology; pending experimental validation.

    Conclusions

    • Malignancy is a network reorganization: denser, ×4.2 clustering, shorter paths, lower modularity.
    • A hyperconnected core, resilient to random hits yet dependent on a few bottlenecks.
    • Rationale for multi-node combination instead of single-target therapy. Next: in-silico multi-hub knock-outs and scRNA-seq PPI networks.

    • HPRD
    • STRING ≥ 700
    • IntNetDB

    Naïve Bayes integration (LRmax rule, posterior P > 0.5) gave a normal network (62 nodes, 62 edges) and a Luminal A network (62 nodes, 77 edges). We measured density, clustering, path length and Louvain modularity, then ran random and targeted (degree, betweenness) removal of 0–80% of nodes, tracking S and Eglob.

    Limitations & scope

    Static topological snapshot built from 62 manually curated genes per network (no differential-expression filtering); PPI graphs omit dynamics, concentrations and PTMs. Findings are hypotheses pending experimental validation.
